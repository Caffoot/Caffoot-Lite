# Caffoot Lite — Public Roadmap

**Everyday IT Management.** Caffoot Lite brings infrastructure, people, equipment and support work together for small businesses, IT teams and home labs. Published v2.1 covers infrastructure inventory; unreleased v2.2 extends it with the connected workflows below.

This page helps visitors see what is available, what is being developed and which ideas are being considered. **An idea is not a promised feature or release date.** The product owner decides priorities and approves changes before development.

## Available now — v2.1

Caffoot Lite v2.1 was published on **8 November 2026**. The current release includes:

- Device inventory with improved physical-server and virtual-machine forms.
- Subnet-first IP address management (IPAM), optional VLANs and guided bulk subnet planning.
- Network rack documentation, front/rear views and equipment placement.
- Multi-user access, administrator-managed permissions, MFA, account/email workflows and Audit Trail.
- A modern, responsive light/dark interface and Docker/SQLite deployment with persistent data.

**[Install Caffoot Lite](INSTALLATION.md)** · **[See the latest release](https://github.com/Caffoot/Caffoot-Lite/releases/latest)** · **[Explore the features](docs/how-tos/features-and-instructions/README.md)**

## In development — v2.2, not published

**Milestone 01 — Everyday IT Management** targets v2.2. Its development features include:

- Shared roles, individual permissions, MFA and optional-feature controls.
- Staff Members, Departments, equipment assignments and My Equipment.
- Optional IT Assets for receipt, preparation, issue, return, Device registration/reissue, associated accessories, handover, quantity corrections and stock reporting.
- Suppliers, Supplier Issues, Purchasing, equipment-supplier associations and Equipment Lifecycle.
- Employee and technician Service Desk workflows, shared-equipment privacy, unread replies, working-time reporting, notification templates and company branding.
- Protected legacy inventory import, unified Staff/login administration and more readable Audit Trail details.

**v2.2 remains unreleased.** These features are integrated in development and are not included in published v2.1. Installation, upgrade and complete-volume recovery for the current candidate still require verification, alongside outstanding native/manual, security and release checks. No release date is promised.

Read the [draft v2.2 guides](docs/how-tos/features-and-instructions/v2.2-draft/README.md) and [draft release notes](docs/Release-Notes-v2.2.md). The [six planning milestones](docs/product-direction/PROPOSED-MILESTONES.md) retain the longer-term context; proposals require their own agreed scope before development.

## Help shape Caffoot Lite

You don't need to write code to contribute. Try the current release, share your experience, tell us what was difficult or suggest something useful.

- **[Suggest a feature or report a bug](https://github.com/Caffoot/Caffoot-Lite/issues/new/choose)** — use one issue per suggestion, so decisions remain easy to follow.
- **[Find ways to contribute](docs/Community-Participation.md)** — testing, documentation and practical feedback are welcome.
- **[Read the contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md)** — our shared standards for welcoming and constructive participation.

Please [report security vulnerabilities privately](SECURITY.md), not in public issues.
