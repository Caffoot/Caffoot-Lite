# Caffoot Lite — Public Roadmap

**Everyday IT Management.** Caffoot Lite brings infrastructure, people, equipment and support work together for small businesses, IT teams and home labs. This branch prepares unreleased v2.2 with the connected workflows below.

This page helps visitors see what is available, what is being developed and which ideas are being considered. **An idea is not a promised feature or release date.** The product owner decides priorities and approves changes before development.

## In development — v2.2, not published

**Milestone 01 — Everyday IT Management** targets v2.2. Its integrated features include:

- Device inventory with improved physical-server and virtual-machine forms.
- Subnet-first IP address management (IPAM), optional VLANs and guided bulk subnet planning.
- Network rack documentation, front/rear views and equipment placement.
- Multi-user access, administrator-managed permissions, MFA, account/email workflows and Audit Trail.
- A modern, responsive light/dark interface and Docker/SQLite deployment with persistent data.
- Shared roles, individual permissions, MFA and optional-feature controls.
- Staff Members, Departments, equipment assignments and My Equipment.
- Optional IT Assets for receipt, preparation, issue, return, Device registration/reissue, associated accessories, handover, quantity corrections and stock reporting.
- Suppliers, Supplier Issues, Purchasing, equipment-supplier associations and Equipment Lifecycle.
- Employee and technician Service Desk workflows, shared-equipment privacy, unread replies, working-time reporting, notification templates and company branding.
- Protected legacy inventory import, unified Staff/login administration and more readable Audit Trail details.

**v2.2 remains unreleased.** These features are integrated in development. Installation, upgrade and complete-volume recovery for the current candidate still require verification, alongside outstanding native/manual, security and release checks. No release date is promised.

Read the [draft v2.2 guides](docs/how-tos/features-and-instructions/v2.2/README.md) and [draft release notes](docs/historical/release-notes/Release-Notes-v2.2.md). The [six planning milestones](docs/product-direction/PROPOSED-MILESTONES.md) retain the longer-term context; proposals require their own agreed scope before development.
