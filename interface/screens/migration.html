Automatic safety backup

Before restoring a backup or clearing your inventory, Caffoot Lite keeps a copy of your previous inventory in this browser. You can download it from Settings if it is available.

Example

If you have 60 devices and restore an older backup with 50, the automatic safety backup protects the previous 60-device inventory.

Important: Browser storage can be cleared or lost, and a later operation may replace the automatic safety backup. Download your own backups and keep them somewhere safe.
