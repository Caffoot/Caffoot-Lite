How Assignments Work

Equipment can be assigned here or from its existing Device/IT Asset record. Both routes update one underlying assignment, with the same equipment identity and history.

Preparation before reissue: returned or transferred equipment needs the checks appropriate to its type. Quantity returns can distinguish cleaned and ready, damaged and written off, and awaiting checks in one transaction.

Access: an optional feature or missing effective section permission must remove its equipment details and actions, including in history. This dropdown demonstrates visibility only; the real backend enforces access.

Functional concept for discussion · Sample data is never saved · Existing approved v2.1 screens remain authoritative.
