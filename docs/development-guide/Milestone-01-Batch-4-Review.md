- Supplier Equipment sums immutable original receipt quantities plus eligible independent Devices; stock movement reports use effective corrected quantities. Those can differ after a recording correction. This is a source-confirmed metric distinction, not a current stock or financial total.
- Stock Inventory has dashboard-derived filters, not a standalone search toolbar. Foundation metadata-correction support is not an exposed Edit Specifications workflow. Correct Movement cannot edit individual identities or dates/recipients/Supplier/specifications and cannot repair an impossible intervening sequence. No application fix is made.
- Supplier Issues are company-owned retained records, with all four status transitions and no generic delete/move; they are not Service Desk tickets. Purchasing has no implemented financial order/payment/approval/replenishment processing. A Supplier Issue resolution has no physical stock effect.

No new unresolved owner product decision was found. Record-specific factual problems outside supported corrections require an administrator/owner decision rather than an invented workaround.
