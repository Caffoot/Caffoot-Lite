### Material Behaviour Confirmed

- `staff_employment.disable_employment_login` and `save_staff_member` keep Staff status, login disablement, session/email/password-change proof revocation and attributed history in one transaction. Staff Members Edit is required; linked status also requires an active MFA-confirmed administrator. Self/last-active-administrator rejection rolls back the whole save. `require_active_staff_login` rejects enabling while Left Company; reactivation does not call account enabling. The source has two employment choices, not a general catalogue of inactive statuses.
- `role_policy.evaluate_role_policy` combines highest enabled role grants then explicit overrides, and requires MFA for administrator status, any enabled additional role or wider effective permissions. Enrolled MFA remains required. `confirm_role_change` revalidates a signed preview; a stale rejection produces refreshed consequences without saving. Membership changes always revoke sessions; other access/MFA effects follow actual impact.
- `permissions.reset_user_password` excludes self and all administrators, keeps disabled accounts disabled and sets mandatory change while revoking sessions/proofs. Promotion requires confirmed MFA, verified nonblank email and completed temporary-password change. Current Make admin UI does not itself enable a disabled login.
- `account_email.confirm_email_proof` cannot bypass required/enrolled MFA; durable five-failure limits survive resend/restart, codes expire after 15 minutes, neutral requests reveal no eligibility. Back clears local fields; it does not claim to revoke a server proof. Recovery from a preceding MFA step returns to it. Initial creation has no mandatory-change flag; assisted reset does.
- My Equipment resolves the signed-in identity without a supplied Staff/account ID and returns only current personal/Department records. Server-projected fields exclude private inventory data; Laptop/Desktop software requires recognised true saved flags. Archived Department membership can retain current shared visibility. A disabled account cannot use authenticated requests. An old visible row does not authorise a later Details request.

Examples retained/checked against these rules: Alex Morgan's Staff-only and linked-account creation, Tim/Dave technician overrides, Alex's MFA/email promotion eligibility, leaving/returning with a disabled login, AC-LAP-07 personal assignment, shared AC-PRN-01 and Priya's Accounts→Sales visibility change.
